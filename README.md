# Ex.No.-1.2-Design and Draft the given 2D Sketches in modelling software.

## AIM
 To sketch the given drawing with dimensions using fusion 360 tool.
 
## REQUIREMENTS
 1. Autodesk fusion 360
 2. Windows 10
 3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
 4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 
 
 ## PROCEDURE
 1.	Open new file and then select sketch icon.
 2.	Select the plane for sketching. 
 3. Draw a normal/construct vertical and horizontal line and fix it along the axis using the fix/unfix tool.

 4. Draw four circles from the center point with the radius of 15 for inner circle,third outer circle 21,second outer circle32, outer circle40 .

 5. Draw a line between the second and third circle where the dimension between the line and the vertical line should be 3 .Mirror the line with the y axis using the mirror tool. Using the circular pattern in the create ,select the mirrored lines and make it in a circular patter of 6 .
 6. Trim the unwanted lines in the circle using the trim tool in the constraint.

 7.  Draw two circles of radius 21mm and radius 15mm in the upside of the center circle with the dimension of 50mm from the vertical line and dimension of 60 with the horizontal line.
 8.  Draw two circle of radius 15mm and 20mm in the left or right side from the center circle with the dimension of 70mm from the center point .
 9.  create a circle of radius 20 and make it tangent with the center circle and the circle of radius 42 made in upside. Add a line in the left side and make it tangent to the circle of radius 21 and 20.

 10.  Trim the extra parts with the triming tool.
 11.  Select the parts in the right side to mirror . First mirror it in the down side.
Then select the full left part and mirror it in the right side , and trim the unwanted parts.
 12. Finish the sketch and extruded it with the diameter of 20mm.
